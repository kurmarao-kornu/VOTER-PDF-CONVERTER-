5. What This Version Checks

  • Each voter-card heading is detected separately using its page position and three-column layout.
  • EPIC Number is stored as text and is the main reference. Repeated voter names are allowed. Duplicate EPICs are flagged, never deleted.
  • Ward, serial number, voter name, father/husband name, house number, locality, gender, age, EPIC, section number, original PDF page and printed roll page are included when readable.
  • Cover total, detected card count, serial gaps, duplicate serials and pages with no detected cards are reported per PDF.
  • Searchable Telugu PDFs in the Andhra Pradesh three-column photo-roll layout are supported. Scanned/image-only PDFs need OCR and are not silently treated as successfully converted.
