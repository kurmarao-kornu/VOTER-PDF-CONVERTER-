What This Version Checks

  • Detects individual voter cards using page position and three-column layout of Andhra Pradesh photo electoral rolls.
  • Extracts EPIC number as primary identifier (stored as text).
  • Pulls Ward No, Serial No, Voter Name, Father/Husband Name, House No, Locality, Gender, Age when present.
  • Flags duplicate EPIC values (never deleted) and serial gaps.
  • Compares detected card count against cover-page total when readable.
  • Supported: Searchable Telugu PDFs in the standard AP three-column layout.
  • Not supported: Purely scanned / image-only PDFs (require OCR; not auto-processed).
Client-side only · No data leaves your browser · For personal use of published electoral rolls
